Reports & ideas

The commanders behind the fixes.

What each of them reported or asked for, on GitHub and on the Frontier Forums.

On GitHub

  • pioniere: the first Linux review, on Nobara under Proton and Wayland, and the silent Test voice (issue 3).
  • docfallingapart: the Linux app-menu entry, and a folder with a space in its name.
  • nhok0169: Ask answering from old data, the empty Services tab, the window freezing with the live feed, the synthesis and chart drawing, long module names, shared missions, and the "where do I get this material" idea (issue 4).
  • hangerhead: a carrier's docking access in the market lists, how long a buy order has been live, and a louder voice.
  • Chequers2: a surface mining map that remembers the body and the site.

On the Frontier Forums

  • cmdr chequers: journals on a network share, the fleet carrier jump and cooldown timers, and the memory comparison with EDDiscovery.
  • Alvarrune: a Steam library on another drive, a colonisation depot that wasn't theirs, the route countdown, and the dimmer crash.
  • Asmodeus: Elite Nexus changing bindings (it never touches them now).
  • Smallfishnz: the window vanishing, which is why there is a crash log.
  • NetworX: the BGS tick hour resetting.
  • BigOldChicken: searching outfitting for a module. Jo Bangles: the first impressions.

Community knowledge

What the app is built on.

Themes, settings, data and research that commanders and projects shared first. Sources and licences are listed in NOTICE.md.

Cockpit

HUD themes by CMDR Diomedian (Empire blue, Federation, Alliance green), CMDR Alex Traut (Royal purple), Arkku (Blue and red, and the HUD colour editor the themes come from) and Prima Games (VR yellow). CMDR Morbad's Odyssey glare settings. 3Dmigoto by bo3b.

Data and research

EDCD's FDevIDs, EDEngineer (msarilar), EDDiscovery, jixxed's journal schemas, Richard Buckle's EDRefCard, the exobiology research of Canonn, EDMC-BioScan, SrvSurvey and EDAstro, and MattG's exploration value formulae. The services Spansh, EDSM, Inara, EDDN and EDAstro. The Inter typeface and Phosphor icons.
