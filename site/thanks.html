Reports & ideas

The commanders behind the fixes.

What each of them reported or asked for, on GitHub and on the Frontier Forums.

On GitHub

  • pioniere: the first Linux review, on Nobara under Proton and Wayland, and the silent Test voice (discussion 17).
  • docfallingapart: the Linux app-menu entry, and a folder with a space in its name.
  • nhok0169: Ask answering from old data, the empty Services tab, the window freezing with the live feed, the synthesis and chart drawing, long module names, shared missions, the "where do I get this material" idea, the shopping trip for several goods, trade routes for another ship's cargo with stock and demand, rolls at each grade, a number of modules, and the whole way to each engineer (issue 4).
  • hangerhead: a carrier's docking access in the market lists, how long a buy order has been live, and a louder voice.
  • Chequers2: a surface mining map that remembers the body and the site, and numbered deposits on the surface radar.
  • vjt960: the neutron planner's ×6 boost for the Caspian's FSD Mk II (issue 19).
  • WadRail: sold exobiology data that stayed listed as unsold, and the journal lines that showed the game logging sales without their samples (issue 20).
  • chrisvfritz: the Merc blueprints that were missing from Engineering (issue 10).
  • nEo-1664: a long unsold exobiology list that pushed the rest of the page down, which now scrolls inside its card (issue 24).
  • yqol: the crash log of a graphics card that reset under Elite Nexus (issue 22).

On the Frontier Forums

  • cmdr chequers: journals on a network share, the fleet carrier jump and cooldown timers, and the memory comparison with EDDiscovery.
  • Alvarrune: a Steam library on another drive, a colonisation depot that wasn't theirs, the route countdown, the dimmer crash, screenshots saved as PNG, and a notice in the app when a new version is out.
  • Asmodeus: Elite Nexus changing bindings (it never touches them now), and clearing the recent mission history.
  • Smallfishnz: the window vanishing, which is why there is a crash log.
  • NetworX: the BGS tick hour resetting, the merits earned in each system, trade routes from the station you're docked at, and Where to mine for the Rhino.
  • BigOldChicken: searching outfitting for a module. Jo Bangles: the first impressions.

Community knowledge

What the app is built on.

Themes, settings and data that commanders and projects shared first. Sources and licences are listed in NOTICE.md.

Cockpit

HUD themes by CMDR Diomedian (Empire blue, Federation, Alliance green), CMDR Alex Traut (Royal purple), Arkku (Blue and red, and the HUD colour editor the themes come from) and Prima Games (VR yellow). CMDR Morbad's Odyssey glare settings. 3Dmigoto by bo3b.

Data

EDCD's FDevIDs, EDEngineer (msarilar), EDDiscovery, jixxed's journal schemas and Richard Buckle's EDRefCard. The services Spansh, EDSM, Inara, EDDN and EDAstro. The Inter typeface and Phosphor icons.
