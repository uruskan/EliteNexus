Cockpit lights

Less cabin light. More of the galaxy.

Turn down the cockpit’s own lamps and glow with a dedicated dimmer. Keep your HUD readable, your surroundings unchanged, and adjust the cabin while you fly.

ON THIS PAGEDimming controlsIn-flight keysInstall & calibrateGood to know

Set the mood of the cabin

Two controls. Only the cockpit’s own lights.

Elite Dangerous has no setting for its cockpit lights. Elite Nexus adds a dimmer through an optional graphics hook: lights within reach of your eyes keep only the share of their light you choose.

Light kept

How much of the cockpit’s light remains. Lower it for a subdued cabin; raise it when you want more of the interior in view. The lamps and their glow change; HUD brightness doesn’t.

Reach

How far from your viewpoint the dimmer reaches. About 5 metres covers most cockpits; a larger reach gives room for a different ship.

Stations, carriers, planets and the HUD stay as they are: the fix changes the game’s light shader for the cockpit’s own lights and nothing else.

Adjust without leaving the game

Three keys, with the game window in front.

Set your starting values in Elite Nexus; they apply the next time the game starts. Then adjust in flight.

F9

Dimmer on or off

Compare the cockpit lighting in place.

F10

Step the light kept

0, 15, 35, 60 and 100 %.

F11

Step the reach

5, 8 and 12 metres.

They work without Shift, Ctrl or Alt held. The game gets every press of these keys too (Elite Nexus can’t hold one back, and never changes your controls), so if the control presets the game uses bind one of them on its own, the tab names the action; if it can’t read those presets, it says so instead of guessing.

One installation, two cockpit features

Install, calibrate once, remove in one click.

The dimmer is 3Dmigoto, an open-source Direct3D 11 hook placed next to the game, plus a fix for the game’s light shader. The same hook lets HUD colours keep hostile red, friendly green and shield blue; either feature works without the other.

  1. Install from the tab

    Install the dimmer first says exactly what will happen, then downloads 3Dmigoto 1.4.9 from its official GitHub release, checks it against pinned checksums and puts its files next to EliteDangerous64.exe. None of the game’s own files is changed, and everything added is listed so it can come out again.

  2. Calibrate once

    Start the game, get into your cockpit somewhere quiet and quit to the desktop. For that one session the game records the shaders it loads (about 21 MB, deleted afterwards); Elite Nexus then finds the light shader among them and keeps a fix for exactly that shader. In the game, 3Dmigoto swaps only those shaders; everything else, including shaders the game adds later, is left alone.

  3. Remove whenever you like

    Remove takes out exactly the files Elite Nexus installed (any that changed since are left in place), the calibration and its fixes. Because the same hook keeps hostile red, friendly green and shield blue, Remove switches that off too.

Good to know

Compatibility, and what happens after a game update.

Can I use the dimmer with EDHM?

No. The hook can’t run alongside EDHM or another 3Dmigoto mod, and the tab says so if one is there. It is an unofficial graphics modification: Frontier tolerates mods like this without having approved them. The fixes are made on your PC from your own game and never leave it.

What happens after a game update?

The tab shows when and for which game build it was calibrated. After an update it asks you to calibrate again, because a changed light shader no longer matches; until then the dimmer is simply off and the game is unaffected.

I installed the dimmer with Elite Nexus 0.3.0. Is that a problem?

Yes: that version’s patterns crash the game near the newer fleet carriers. Elite Nexus says so at start while that hook is in place, and Update and calibrate replaces it (it also puts back a d3d11.dll that was renamed to switch the hook off).

Does it work on Linux and on a Mac?

It is untested there. Proton, CrossOver and Whisky bring their own Direct3D 11 and may skip the hook in the game folder; if calibrating finds no shaders, add WINEDLLOVERRIDES="d3d11,d3dcompiler_47=n,b" %command% to the game’s launch options in Steam. If it still finds none, the hook can’t run there, and Remove takes it out again.
