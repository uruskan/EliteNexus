# Fleet carriers

## Owning a carrier
A fleet carrier costs about five billion credits and has a weekly upkeep that grows with the services installed. The carrier's bank pays the upkeep; if it runs out, the carrier eventually gets decommissioned, so keep its balance topped up. Carrier names and callsigns (XXX-XXX) are visible to everyone.

## Jumping
A carrier jumps up to 500 ly per jump after a 15-minute countdown (longer when the servers are busy; the game sets the departure time), followed by a 5-minute cooldown before it can jump again. Elite Nexus counts both down live on the Fleet carrier page, next to Navigation > Fleet carrier, and on the Dashboard. Each jump burns tritium from the carrier's fuel tank (more for longer jumps and for a heavier carrier with lots of cargo). Plan long trips with the fleet carrier planner (Navigation > Fleet carrier in Elite Nexus), which counts tritium and where to refuel. Tritium is mined in icy-ring hotspots or bought; the market tools find the cheapest tritium near the carrier.

## Trading with a carrier
Carriers have a market: the owner sets buy orders (the carrier buys commodities) and sell orders. Haulers fill buy orders for profit; owners use carriers to move large cargo or to collect mining output. Some carriers post very high buy prices for rare salvage (for example Thargoid parts). Carrier prices can change at any moment and carriers move, so fresh data matters.

## Squadron carriers and decommissioning
Since Vanguards (August 2025) a squadron can own a squadron carrier: bigger (60,000 t capacity), shared by the squadron, and members with the right rank can manage it and schedule its jumps. Elite Nexus tracks it apart from the commander's own carrier and shows both on the Fleet carrier page (a switch picks one), the Dashboard, the Galaxy map and the Navigation fleet carrier planner. The journal only records what the commander does themselves: jumps other members schedule and orders they set aren't in it, so the squadron carrier's position updates when the game next reports it, and its stats when the commander next opens its carrier management screen.
Decommissioning a carrier takes a while: the game sets a scrap time, and until then the owner can cancel in carrier management. At the scrap time the carrier is gone and the refund is paid. Elite Nexus shows the scrap date and refund while it's pending, and afterwards says the carrier was decommissioned instead of showing it as live.

## Services and access
Services can include refuel, repair, rearm, shipyard, outfitting, Universal Cartographics, Vista Genomics, a bartender, redemption office and secure storage. Docking access can be open to all, friends, squadron members or nobody.

## Getting back to the carrier, and carrier administration
Navigation > Fleet carrier in Elite Nexus shows where the commander's carrier is (the system the game last reported) and how far that is, with "Route my ship here", which plots every jump there for the current ship at once (the exact plotter, or the neutron plotter when the ship's loadout isn't known). The Fleet carrier page has the same button. The same tab lists the stations with the Fleet Carrier Administration service nearest the carrier or the commander, from Spansh's station search, with one-click routes there for the ship or the carrier. Spansh lists several hundred such stations, mostly large starports.
