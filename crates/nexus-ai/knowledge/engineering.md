# Engineering

## Engineers
Engineers modify ship modules (and, in Odyssey, suits and weapons) with blueprints of grades 1 to 5. Each engineer is unlocked in steps. First the commander learns of them. Five ship engineers are known from the start (Felicity Farseer, Elvira Martuuk, The Dweller, Liz Ryder, Tod 'The Blaster' McQuinn), and every other ship engineer is referred by one once the commander reaches grade 3 and a third of the way to grade 4 with them. Six on-foot engineers are known from the start too (Domino Green, Hero Ferrari, Jude Navarro, and Baltanos, Eleanor Bresa and Rosa Dayette in Colonia); each other one comes from a referral task, goods handed to the engineer before. Then the commander earns the invitation: a requirement such as a rank, a reputation, or something mined, traded or sold. Ship engineers then want something handed in at their workshop; on-foot engineers don't. Selene Jean, for example: grade 3 and a third of grade 4 with Tod McQuinn, 500 tons of ore mined, then 10 Painite. Rank with an engineer grows as blueprints are crafted and opens higher grades. Each engineer works on specific module types up to a maximum grade. Experimental effects (for example Mass Manager on a frame shift drive, Thermal Spread on weapons) can be added once a module is engineered. Elite Nexus' Engineering page lists blueprints, the materials needed for a wishlist, and each engineer with the whole way to them and the step the commander is on.

## Rolls
A roll is one craft at the engineer, using one set of the grade's materials. Since the game's Update 18.08 (August 2024) each roll fills a fixed share of its grade, set by the grade and the commander's rank with the engineer, so luck no longer comes into it. At an engineer's top rank (5), grade 1 takes 1 roll, grade 2 takes 2 and so on up to 5 rolls for grade 5. Below the top rank, commanders report 5 rolls at the rank that opens a grade and one fewer for each rank above. Older guides that speak of random rolls and 10 or more rolls for a grade 5 predate that update. The Blueprint planner starts from the top rank's counts, lets the commander change them for each grade, and plans several modules at once.

## Materials
Three kinds, each with grades 1 (very common) to 5 (very rare):
- Raw: mined from planet surfaces (surface prospecting, crystal structures, geological sites) and asteroids.
- Manufactured: salvaged from ship wrecks, signal sources, combat, mission rewards.
- Encoded (data): scanning ships, wakes and data points, settlement terminals.
Material storage is limited per grade (grade 1 holds the most).

## Material traders
Raw, manufactured and encoded material traders at stations exchange materials of their kind: trading up one grade costs six of the lower grade; trading down one grade gives three of the lower grade; trading sideways within the same kind costs extra. Elite Nexus computes the exact trades for a wishlist (Engineering > Planner) and the market tools find the nearest trader of each kind.

## Technology brokers
Human and Guardian technology brokers unlock special modules and weapons in exchange for materials and commodities (Guardian modules need Guardian materials from Guardian sites).

## Synthesis
Synthesis turns materials into useful items without docking: FSD injections (jump boosts), ammunition, limpets, AFM (repair) and SRV refuels and repairs, among others.

## Odyssey suits and weapons
Suits and handheld weapons are upgraded in grade (class 1 to 5) at Pioneer Supplies and modified by Odyssey engineers with on-foot materials: goods, assets and data found in settlements, from missions and from bartenders. Elite Nexus' Engineering page shows suit and weapon upgrades and what the ship locker holds.
