Themes, settings and data that commanders and projects shared first. Sources and licences are listed in NOTICE.md.

HUD themes by CMDR Diomedian (Empire blue, Federation, Alliance green), CMDR Alex Traut (Royal purple), Arkku (Blue and red, and the HUD colour editor the themes come from) and Prima Games (VR yellow). CMDR Morbad's Odyssey glare settings. 3Dmigoto by bo3b.

Data

EDCD's FDevIDs, EDEngineer (msarilar), EDDiscovery, jixxed's journal schemas and Richard Buckle's EDRefCard. The services Spansh, EDSM, Inara, EDDN and EDAstro. The Inter typeface and Phosphor icons.
