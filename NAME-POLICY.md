# The Elite Nexus name and icon

Elite Nexus is free software: anyone may run, study, change and share it under the GNU General Public License
(`LICENSE`, with the additional terms in `LICENSE-ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md`). The code is yours to build on. The name is
not part of that: "Elite Nexus" and its icon are how commanders tell which program they are running, who made it and
where to report a problem, so they stay with the original.

This page is not part of the licence. It says how the author, CMDR Alka Uruskan, asks the name and icon to be used,
and what the author will rely on under the law on trademarks and unfair competition where it applies.

## Fine without asking

* Sharing unchanged copies of the official releases, under their own name.
* Building Elite Nexus from unchanged source, or packaging it for a distribution with only the changes needed to
  build or install it there, under its own name.
* Saying truthfully what your work is: "based on Elite Nexus", "a fork of Elite Nexus", "works with Elite Nexus".
* Writing about Elite Nexus, reviewing it, linking to it and showing screenshots of it.

## Please don't

* Give a changed version the name "Elite Nexus" or "EliteNexus", or a name built from it: "Elite Nexus 2",
  "EliteNexus-Gen2", "Elite Nexus Next", "Elite Nexus Plus", "Elite Nexus Reborn" and the like. Commanders read those as the next
  official version.
* Use the Elite Nexus icon or logo for a changed version.
* Present a changed version as the original, as official, or as made or endorsed by the author.

A changed version needs a name and an icon of its own, and a line that says it is based on Elite Nexus.

## Why

A fork under the same name sends its bug reports, its mistakes and its support questions to the original, and a
commander can't tell which program wrote to their game folder or uploaded their data. A name of its own protects the
fork's users as much as the original's.

If you would like to use the name in a way this page doesn't cover, open an issue at
<https://github.com/uruskan/EliteNexus/issues> and ask.
